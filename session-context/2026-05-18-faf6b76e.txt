## Multi-source ownership verification (proposed)

Three-tier escalation. Each tier is cheap when it works; we fall back when it doesn't.

### Tier 1 — Google Maps owner-verified flag
On profile claim/onboard, take the `place_id` and call Places Details. Google exposes a `business_status` and (via the Business Profile API for verified owners only) an owner-status signal. If the email/phone the member gave us matches what Google has, we have soft proof.

**Cost:** $17/1000 calls. **Strength:** Strong when present, but most signals require the actual Business Profile API which needs OAuth from the owner — circular.

### Tier 2 — Website cross-check
If they claimed `mayasbakery.com`, scrape it (we already use Jina Reader for this). Look for: their claimed business name, the same phone/email, the same Instagram handle. If 3+ overlap, we have strong correlation that the website, the Google listing, and the chat are all describing the same business.

**Cost:** Free. **Strength:** Strong correlation but doesn't prove the *person* is the owner — just that the *data* is consistent.

### Tier 3 — Grounded LLM search (Gemini grounding / Perplexity)
For everything that doesn't have a website or Google Maps presence (an artist, a community organizer, a new pop-up), ask a search-grounded model: *"Is there evidence Maya runs a bakery called Maya's in Fruitvale Oakland? Cite sources."* Gemini's grounded-search mode and Perplexity both return cited results.

**Cost:** ~$0.01–0.05/lookup. **Strength:** Catches the long tail. Won't catch determined fraud, but raises the bar enough that casual impersonators give up.

### Confidence score, not gate

| **Enriching** | URL captured (`websiteUrl`/`googleMapsUrl`/etc.) + not yet enriched | Jina Reader scrapes site + Google Places lookup → GPT extracts new fields → merges (only fills gaps) |
| **Verifying** | ≥2 contact channels (website / GMaps / IG / phone) + not yet verified | Gemini grounded check — "do these all describe the same business?" → writes `ownershipVerification` with confidence 0–1 |

All happen in the background after the bot's reply is sent. No user-visible delay.
